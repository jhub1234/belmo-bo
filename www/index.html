🦌 Wild Haven

Protecting habitats, one acre at a time.

Our Mission

Wild Haven partners with local communities to restore wetlands and forests, giving native wildlife room to thrive. Every project is monitored, measured, and shared openly.

What we do

Habitat restoration, species monitoring, and public education programs across three regions. Volunteers plant native trees and track recovery each season.

Get involved

Join a field day, adopt an acre, or simply follow along. Reach out through your regional chapter to learn more.
